Welcome to ResultVue. We are dedicated to providing educational institutions with a seamless, fast, and secure exam result management portal. By subscribing to and utilizing our platform, you agree to the following terms, designed to protect both your institution and our systems.

1. Infrastructure & Data Hosting Consent

To ensure high-speed performance and strictly enforce the limits of your chosen subscription plan, ResultVue provisions and hosts dedicated Google Sheets on our secure Google Workspace infrastructure. By using our service, the Institution explicitly acknowledges and consents to ResultVue hosting this academic data on its servers to generate the results portal.

  • Your Access: Institutions are granted managed editing access to these Sheets strictly for entering student data according to the purchased plan.
  • Data Ownership: The Institution remains the sole owner (Data Controller) of the entered data. ResultVue acts only as the technology provider (Data Processor).
2. Data Retention & Deletion

We respect your data privacy and maintain strict data hygiene practices. ResultVue does not hold your data indefinitely.

  • Post-Expiry Deletion: If a subscription expires or is terminated, ResultVue reserves the right to permanently delete the institution's databases and all associated records within 30 days after the expiry date.
  • On-Request Deletion: An institution can request the complete and immediate deletion of their data at any point in time.
  • Note: We highly recommend that institutions regularly download and keep local offline backups of their result sheets.
3. Fair Usage & Zero-Tolerance Abuse Policy

ResultVue subscriptions are tailored based on the number of classes or students. To protect the integrity of our platform, the following rules are strictly enforced:

Any attempt to bypass subscription limits (e.g., adding unauthorized classes to the database), alter the internal structure of the provided Google Sheets, share editing access with unauthorized third parties, or exploit our API endpoints is strictly prohibited.

Consequences of Abuse: Detection of such activities will result in immediate account termination without any refund, and ResultVue reserves the right to initiate legal proceedings for data breaches or system manipulation.

ResultVue relies on robust third-party infrastructure (Google LLC). While we strive for 99.9% uptime, we cannot be held legally liable for unforeseen service downtimes, Google policy changes, or minor technical glitches. At ResultVue, data security isn't just a feature; it's the foundation of our architecture. This policy explains how we handle institutional and student data.

1. Data Processing Roles

In accordance with data protection guidelines:

  • The Institution is the "Data Controller": You decide what student marks are uploaded and published. By utilizing our platform, the Institution guarantees it has the requisite authority and parental consent (where applicable) to process and publish minors' academic data.
  • ResultVue is the "Data Processor": We simply provide the secure digital pipeline that takes your data and displays it beautifully to your students.
Transient Caching for Maximum Privacy: ResultVue never permanently stores student result queries on external web servers. When a student searches for their result, the data is pulled directly from the secured Google Sheet into a temporary, encrypted RAM cache that auto-erases after a maximum of 6 hours.
2. Information Collection & Security

We restrict data collection to the absolute minimum required to operate the portal:

  • Admin Data: We securely store the Institution Name, Admin Contact Details, and Subscription Validity in our Master Control Panel to authenticate your dashboard access.
  • Database Security: Each institution's database is sandboxed. It is technologically impossible for a student or admin of 'School A' to query or access the database of 'School B'.
3. No Third-Party Data Sharing

ResultVue operates on a strict zero-sharing policy. We do not sell, trade, rent, or distribute any administrative details or student academic information to third-party marketing networks, analytics firms, or external entities under any circumstances.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

We use localized browser storage strictly for administrative authentication (keeping you logged in securely). We do not deploy invasive tracking cookies or targeted advertising pixels on the student result portal.
